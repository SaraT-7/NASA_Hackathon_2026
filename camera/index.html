About this model

A segmentation network (DeepLabv3 + MobileNetV3) trained on AI4Mars: Curiosity Navcam photos labelled pixel by pixel by volunteers for NASA JPL.

Tested on 322 images labelled by NASA rover drivers and scientists, it finds 90 % of the sand they marked, and 95 % of what it calls sand really is sand.

It runs here, in your browser (onnxruntime-web, WebAssembly). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Limits

Experimental: not used in route planning. Trained on Curiosity Navcam images only; other cameras, colour photos, the sky and ground beyond about 30 m are not reliable, so by default only the lower half of the photo (the near field) is shown and counted. Parts of the rover (arm, deck, wheels) were left out of the training labels, so the model often mistakes them for sand. It does not detect rocks: the training data has too few labelled rocks.
